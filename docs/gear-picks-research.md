## Separate accessory, nested in M1 card

replacement for a silicone strap, not a slip-on cover. Modern candidate, not an
identified accessory from the author’s spare Oceanic.

fitted a spare Oceanic mask to a diver underwater; the soft headpiece helped with

“Как меч у самурая! Может пригодиться только один раз!” The short English passage
is explicitly draft editorial copy, not a translated quotation attributed to him.

## Availability and configuration limits

All eight parent listings and both component listings were retrieved. None was
checked through a destination/size-specific basket. G2 showed “Notify me”; its
manufacturer page also showed out-of-stock for the displayed variant. Navigator
Lite retrieval contained Notify me and generic Add to basket fields together;
current size/colour stock is therefore unconfirmed. Other listings also contain
generic dynamic fields; no exact stock claim is made for them.

Manufacturer colours are range-level options, NOT a claim that the linked Diveinn
SKU offers every illustrated colour. Trinidad retail link is black; GO Sport link
is pink. Palette preview is a visual moodboard only. BCD coral is not described as
a turquoise/pink BCD. No gender-based fit or performance assumptions are made.

The modern spool listing gives 100 ft and a stainless clip, matching the current
manufacturer’s line-length description; do not infer the author’s line length,
spool body material or ownership of that SKU. SMB dimensions/inflation method are
not stated in the UI because the exact current construction was not resolved to
the author’s model. Operational cautions are editorial judgments, not manufacturer
promises of visibility or safety.

## Image rights decision

Real product images were found on product pages. No licence authorising their
republication by this Hub was confirmed. Johnson Outdoors’ website terms reserve
rights and do not grant commercial copying of site content:
https://www.johnsonoutdoors.com/us/terms-and-conditions (updated 2026-09-01).
Publicly accessible media URLs are not treated as permission. No Hollis image
permission was found either; absence of a licence is not filled with an assumption.

No retailer/manufacturer photos were downloaded, hotlinked or republished.
Instead, original SVG category schematics were created from simple independent
geometry, not traced from photos. They are labelled on every card as schematics,
not model photos, with approximate colour swatches. No dimensions, construction
accuracy or exact colour rendering should be inferred. Real-image fields remain
null. Later replacement needs documented permission for the asset and intended use.
